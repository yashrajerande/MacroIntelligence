Investment Cycle Accelerates — Inflation Threatens to Pull the Handbrake

The facts:

Bottom line: Watch core sector YoY: if it falls below 4.0% YoY for two consecutive months, the capex narrative loses its physical corroboration.

Bottom line: If US 10Y yield crosses 5.30% yield again while BOJ holds at 1.25% p.a., the yen carry unwind accelerates — hedge EM duration and INR exposure.

Bottom line: Watch monthly NFO collections: a second consecutive print below ₹1,000 cr / month signals retail distribution is losing momentum and DII absorption capacity is at risk.

NRI Bid Rising — Top End Supplied

The facts:

  • Affordable (< ₹40 lakh) took 16% of sales vs 14% of launches — supply and demand matched
  • City momentum: Hyderabad leads at 15% sales YoY; Chennai trails at -10%
  • NRI buying is RISING: 20% of residential purchases (+10 pp vs prior period in source); 25% within premium/luxury; concentrated in Mumbai Metropolitan Region, Bengaluru, Hyderabad
  • Office: Bengaluru leads leasing at 5.2 mn sq ft / quarter (11% YoY); GCCs take 41% of leasing

The tension: Supply is following demand into the same bands with a lag; the balance shifts one quarter after the sales mix does.

Bottom line: Track the launch share of the undersupplied bands next quarter — supply catching up is what turns a price story into an inventory story.
