India Macro Regime

Global Macro Regime

US GDP at 0.7% SAAR — down from 4.4%, a 370bps collapse — is the number global equity markets are choosing to ignore: S&P 500 at a 100th-percentile 7,708 and Nikkei at a 100th-percentile 65,326 are pricing a growth cycle the underlying economies have stopped delivering. Euro Stoxx at a 100th-percentile 6,444 with Eurozone GDP at 0.9% and CPI re-accelerating to 2.5% from 1.9% is the same trapdoor in a different currency. Global equities are priced for a soft landing; the macro data is delivering something closer to a stall.

Third, the US GDP-equity divergence — 0.7% SAAR growth against S&P at all-time highs — resolves either through a growth recovery (unlikely given core PCE at 3.1% constraining Fed cuts) or an equity correction that triggers FII outflows from India at a moment when DII absorption is thin.
