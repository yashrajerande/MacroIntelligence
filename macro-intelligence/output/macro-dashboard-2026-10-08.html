India Macro Regime

7.8% GDP, 6.5% GVA — Services Papering Over a Softening Goods Economy

The facts:

  • India GDP at 7.8% YoY (64th percentile); GVA growth estimated near 6.5% YoY — the 130 bps gap is statistical discrepancy, not real output.

Bottom line: Watch core sector YoY: a print below 4.5% YoY in the next release would confirm the goods-economy divergence is widening, not closing.

NRI Bid Rising — Top End Supplied

The facts:

  • Affordable (< ₹40 lakh) took 16% of sales vs 14% of launches — supply and demand matched
  • City momentum: Hyderabad leads at 15% sales YoY; Chennai trails at -10%
  • NRI buying is RISING: 20% of residential purchases (+5 pp vs prior period in source); 25% within premium/luxury; concentrated in Mumbai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad
  • Office: Hyderabad leads leasing at 5.3 mn sq ft / quarter (47% YoY); GCCs take 41% of leasing

The tension: Supply is following demand into the same bands with a lag; the balance shifts one quarter after the sales mix does.

Bottom line: Track the launch share of the undersupplied bands next quarter — supply catching up is what turns a price story into an inventory story.
